# Golden pattern: the one hat circuit. The open hat rings and the next closed
# hit chokes it; CH and OH on the same step play open; a ride and a crash
# under it (the shared CR/RD accent DAC: their accents differ per hit).
tempo    140
pattern  I-1-1
name     golden hats
length   16
scale    16
CH  x--- x-x- ---- x-xX
OH  --x- ---x x--- --x-
RD  x-x- x-x- X-x- x-x-
CR  x--- ---- ---- ----
AC  ---- ---- x--- ----
